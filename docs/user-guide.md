# InstrumentHub user guide

Sign in with your individual account. The calendar opens on the first instrument; use the instrument selector and month controls to navigate. Choose **All instruments** for a compact, read-only monthly overview, where each instrument's daily blocks are grouped into one or two lines. Select an individual instrument to hold and book time. State labels and patterns distinguish confirmed, tentative, active, paused, maintenance, outage, and temporarily held time.

To book, select an available fixed block, choose the consecutive block count, and begin the hold. Complete the PI, project, optional grant, experiment, duration, and sample information before the countdown expires. A compliant request confirms immediately; an exception remains tentative and continues to protect its slots.

Open a booking to see its schedule, handler, authenticated-member contact details, and status. Owners may edit or cancel eligible future tentative or confirmed bookings. Contact an administrator for active, paused, completed, rejected, historical, or administratively changed bookings.

Use Inbox to review booking confirmations, approval decisions, cancellations, and applied schedule changes. The calendar identifies the currently scheduled instrument handler and their authenticated-member contact details; it explicitly says when nobody is currently scheduled.
