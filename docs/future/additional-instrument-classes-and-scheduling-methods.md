# Additional instrument classes and scheduling methods

**Goals:** Give each kind of equipment an appropriate calendar while helping users compare instruments that share the same scheduling pattern. Keep unrelated equipment types separate so a mass spectrometer schedule does not become mixed with hourly microscope or tissue-culture bookings.

Future releases may allow administrators to add instrument classes with their own calendars and class-level views. Users could then consider all instruments in one class together without treating every instrument in the laboratory as part of one schedule.

The scheduling system may eventually support hourly, time-of-day, and day-based methods. Hourly equipment may benefit from a focused seven-day calendar, while time-of-day equipment can continue using named daily blocks. The details of those experiences should be revisited when additional equipment types are ready to be supported.
